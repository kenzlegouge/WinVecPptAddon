Poppler (pdftocairo) for Windows, used by the PdfDrop add-in to convert PDF pages to SVG.

Source:  https://github.com/oschwartz10612/poppler-windows (release v26.09.0-0)
Upstream: https://poppler.freedesktop.org/
License: GNU General Public License v2 or v3 (see https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/poppler/poppler/-/blob/master/COPYING).
Bundled third-party libraries (cairo, freetype, fontconfig, ICU, libcurl, OpenSSL, libpng, libjpeg, OpenJPEG, libtiff, zlib, zstd, ...) are under their own licenses.

Only pdftocairo.exe and the DLLs it needs are included here (bin\), plus poppler's encoding data (share\poppler).
If this folder is missing, install.ps1 downloads the latest release and recreates it.
